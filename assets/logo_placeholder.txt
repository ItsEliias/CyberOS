CYBERLAB COMPANION — Logo Placement Instructions
=================================================

Place your custom logo image here as:

  assets/logo.png

Requirements:
- Format: PNG (recommended), JPG, or WEBP
- Recommended dimensions: at least 180×180px (square or near-square works best)
- The app will display it at:
    • 180px max-width on the splash screen (auto-height, aspect ratio preserved)
    • 24px height in the top navigation bar (aspect ratio preserved)
    • Full size on the Progress screen header

If no logo.png is found, the app will automatically display a styled "CC" text mark
in the accent colour of the active theme — no configuration needed.

This assets/ folder is per-app. Each app in the CyberTools suite has its own
assets/logo.png so you can customise branding independently for each tool.

Example logo ideas:
- The ItsEliias armoured knight illustration
- A stylised "CC" monogram
- A shield/hacking icon in your colour palette

After placing the file, restart the app to see your logo appear.
