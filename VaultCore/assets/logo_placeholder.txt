VAULTCORE — Logo Placement Instructions
============================================

Place your custom logo image here:
  assets/logo.png

Requirements:
- Format: PNG (transparent background recommended)
- Minimum size: 256×256px
- No maximum — the app scales it automatically
- Square or near-square aspect ratios work best

How it's used:
- Splash screen: max 180px wide, centred on white background
- Top bar: 24px height, preserves aspect ratio
- Taskbar / dock icon: system-scaled from this file

If no logo.png is found:
- Splash screen shows styled "VC" text mark
- Top bar shows "VC" placeholder in accent colour

This app (VAULTCORE) uses its OWN logo independently
from any other CyberLab tools — drop your chosen PNG here.

Example filenames that work:
  logo.png          ← required name
  (rename yours to logo.png before placing here)
